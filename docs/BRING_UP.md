**Revision A bench qualification worksheet**

**Scope: Rev A.** [Revision overview](https://github.com/barkinvar/candi-clone/blob/main/REV_A.md) · [All revisions](https://github.com/barkinvar/candi-clone/blob/main/REVISIONS.md).

No physical board has been assembled or tested in this project session. Every item below is **pending** until someone records actual measurements. Start with the robot disconnected and use a current-limited bench supply. Save scope captures, board serial number, firmware hash, ambient temperature, load and instrument settings with the results.

| Stage | Procedure | Initial acceptance target / action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unpowered inspection | Check every IC pin 1, diode/capacitor polarity, U3 exposed-pad process, connector joints and jumper orientation against drawings. | No bridges, lifted pins, reversed parts or solder debris. All selectors and JP1/JP2 initially open. |
| Continuity | Verify J1 positive→F1→Q1 drain; Q1 source→VIN_PROT; grounds; CAN H/L connector continuity; each port's power/signal separation. | Match schematic. Rails must not show a persistent near-short to ground after capacitors charge from the meter. Resistance values alone do not prove correct assembly. |
| First input power | Set 12 V, initially 100 mA limit, no sensors/CAN/debug power injection. Apply J1 power and observe current and rail rise. | If current limit persists, power off and investigate; do not simply increase it. A correctly unconfigured board is expected to be well below the conservative 150 mA logic budget. |
| Internal rails | Measure TP2 VIN_PROT, TP3 V5_INTERNAL, TP4 3V3 relative to TP1 GND. | At room temperature, nominal about input minus small loss, 5.023 V, and 3.3 V. Initial DC targets 4.922–5.176 V internal 5 V, 3.201–3.399 V logic. Confirm startup has no damaging overshoot. |
| Default output state | Measure TP5 SENSOR_5V and TP6 SENSOR_VBAT with unconfigured firmware. | Enables low, rails off. Allow bleed resistors to discharge capacitors; a high-impedance meter may show leakage, so repeat with a modest test load. |
| SWD and firmware | Identify STM32F042K6, program supplied image, verify flash, read UART INFO. | Correct image and identity; configured=0 until explicit SET; no unexpected reset loop. |
| Clock/CAN timing | Observe a buffered timing output or CAN bit timing without loading the crystal directly; verify oscillator startup with a suitable low-capacitance method. | CAN 1 Mbit/s, stable startup over input/temperature. Characterize crystal drive ≤100 µW and frequency error; adjust R20/load capacitors only after analysis. |
| Rail enable, no load | Provision unique ID, first rails=0, then deliberately enable one rail at a time. | Correct rail responds, other remains off; UART/CAN state agrees with commanded enables. |
| Steady sensor loading | Electronic loads at 0, 50, 150, 300 mA on each rail, then both at 300 mA. Test J4–J7 individually and distributed loads. | Regulated port 5 V target 4.75–5.25 V steady at declared conditions; document battery drop. Logic remains healthy. Record connector and switch temperatures. |
| Battery sweep | Repeat loads at 6, 7, 9, 12, 14.4 and 18 V input; sweep down/up slowly through reset region. | No CAN lockup or spurious rail enable. Establish actual 5 V dropout and restart limits. Do not claim 6 V full-load regulation until measured. |
| Load steps | Scope internal/port 5 V and 3V3 for 0↔300 mA and mixed-rail steps; include real cable inductance. | Determine transient envelope and settling. A first-order estimate predicts about 0.10 V transient excursion; the ±5% steady target is not a guaranteed transient rating. |
| Current limit | Increase each electronic load beyond 300 mA while monitoring current, voltage, fault and enables. | Approximate 5 V limit 394–506 mA; battery limit 428–600 mA only where VS−OUT≥2.5 V. Confirm hardware latch-off and no automatic cycling. |
| Short and recovery | Use a controlled low-inductance short fixture on each rail, one at a time; capture current and time. Remove short, wait ≥100 ms, explicitly clear. | Battery nominal hardware delay about 11 ms; firmware may shut down earlier on measured output loss; 5 V overload deglitch specified 5–10 ms. Logic/CAN should remain operational. Verify a persistent short cannot be enabled indefinitely through software recovery. |
| Sensor startup | Enable all intended real sensors together at 6, 12 and 18 V where sensors permit. Include maximum cable/capacitance. | No nuisance latch; current and startup delay acceptable. Large capacitive loads may fail despite acceptable steady current. |
| Input thresholds | Use a current-limited adjustable signal source with common ground; ramp each channel up/down in both threshold modes at 6, 12, 18 V board input. | Logic: definite LOW at 0.8 V, HIGH at 2.4 V. Industrial: LOW at 4 V, HIGH at 7 V. Record actual hysteresis. Repeat at temperature endpoints. |
| NPN/PNP/leakage | Emulate NPN/PNP with off leakage up to 0.5 mA, ON residual voltage at intended bound, and actual sensor cable. Test 3.3 V open-drain with 50 µA off leakage. | Correct released/asserted states, all channel combinations, no cross-channel toggles. Check low-battery sensor behavior separately. |
| Filtering | Apply 49 µs glitches, then valid transitions at random sample phases; vary debounce 1, 2, 10, 100 ms. | Glitches rejected for characterized conditions; edge count and state follow intended debounce. Measure latency, do not infer high-speed encoder support. |
| CAN network | Connect a correctly terminated bench bus with another active node to provide ACK. Inspect H/L waveforms, errors and packet timing; then test roboRIO. | Correct ID/payload/rates, no dominant lockup, stable error counters, expected bus load. Confirm disconnect produces unavailable robot data within 100 ms. |
| Firmware stack watermark | In an instrumented test build, fill unused stack space with a pattern, exercise normal traffic, command floods and fault paths, then inspect the deepest overwrite. | Confirm adequate margin; the static 432-byte estimate is not a measured high-water mark. Preserve the test image hash. |
| Fault injections | Disconnect crystal only using a controlled test method, stall foreground in a test image, force watchdog reset, vary logic supply through PVD threshold. | Sensor enables fall; watchdog reset latches rails off; explicit recovery required. Do not short live MCU pins to simulate faults. |
| FRC Broadcast Disable | Send extended data ID 0 on a bench CAN network, including DLC 0 and 8. | Both rails off and latched; diagnostics continue; no automatic restart. Normal disabled robot state may retain sensor power. |
| Configuration interruption | On a dedicated test board, interrupt power at several points in a SET flash write, then reset. | Either old or new valid configuration loads; invalid records never enable an arbitrary rail/ID. Preserve captures and flash dumps. |
| Reverse polarity | With no external powered connections, apply −6 then −12 V through a conservative current limit for a short controlled test. | Protected rail remains unpowered; no heating or damage. Do not apply reverse voltage to sensor SIGNAL/POWER pins. |
| Unpowered inputs | Board off; apply supported positive sensor signals through a current-limited source; measure 3V3 and terminal leakage. | No unintended startup/back-powering, no damage; establish measured leakage. |
| Hot-plug | After basic operation, use a protected test fixture and actual harness at 6/12/18 V. Scope input current, VIN_PROT, Q1 VGS/VDS and rail overshoot. | Validate MOSFET/body-diode pulse stress, fuse survival and TVS energy. The passive model's ~113 A brief peak is not a qualified component stress result. |
| Thermal and environment | At maximum simultaneous load, soak at intended ambient, measure U1/U2/U3/U4/Q1/L1/C1 and connectors; then vibration/cable strain/EMI tests. | Junction and component temperatures remain below rated limits with margin. Establish a real operating-temperature rating; none has yet been qualified. |

Use TP7–TP10 for comparator outputs INPUT1–INPUT4. Use short ground springs for switching-waveform probes. Avoid long probe ground leads that can invent ringing or short adjacent pads. Do not connect an earth-referenced scope ground to a non-ground node. A differential probe is appropriate for Q1 gate-source and floating switch measurements.

**Release gates before robot deployment**

At minimum, complete polarity/continuity, rail regulation over actual battery range, load and short tests, all sensor modes, CAN/roboRIO freshness, reset/watchdog behavior, real-sensor startup and thermal checks. Record any reduced operating range discovered. Industrial EMC surge/EFT/ESD compliance and formal FRC approval are not established by this design or its simulations. Keep the first prototype accessible for measurements and repair.

Suggested measurement log columns: date, board serial, firmware SHA256, test name, input voltage, rail loads, sensor/model/cable, ambient, measured min/max, scope file, pass/fail, corrective action. A CSV template is provided in `reports/bench_measurements_template.csv`.

The [fillable test plan](../reports/bench_test_plan.csv) lists every stage above with status PENDING and blank result/evidence fields. Copy it for each physical board and retain failed results alongside any corrective action. The separate measurement template supports multiple readings and operating conditions per stage.

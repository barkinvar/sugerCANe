# Rev H SC12 assembly

Factory: fit only the 129 SMT references in [Factory_BOM.csv](../bom/Factory_BOM.csv) using [CPL_JLCPCB.csv](../output/assembly/CPL_JLCPCB.csv). User: buy the MCU and [ten through-hole parts](../bom/Hand_solder_BOM.csv) and [three closed-top caps](../bom/ACCESSORIES.csv).

Use the exact [BOM](../bom/BOM_grouped.csv), [assembly drawing](../output/assembly/top_assembly.pdf), [interactive BOM](../output/assembly/interactive_bom.html) and placement files. Reference labels are in these drawings; dense placement does not leave room to print every reference legibly on the actual top silkscreen.

1. Order the 52 × 74 mm, four-layer, 1.6 mm board with 1 oz finished copper target. Use the exported Gerbers and separate plated/nonplated drill files. Have the fabricator review its actual stackup, annular rings, mask registration and minimum features. Use Standard PCBA with the new high-reflow C1 and its specified dwell limits; see [factory instructions](FACTORY_ORDER.md). ENIG is retained for the fine-pitch SMT pads; hand solder the service header after reflow.
2. **Factory:** check bare-board continuity and orientation. Apply paste only to the factory-fitted SMT pads. U5 must be empty and unpasted; use the factory-specific paste layer from the order ZIP. A 0.10 mm stencil is a starting point; the assembler must review release and the VSSOP pad apertures. Through-hole header pads, the termination solder bridge and the test point deliberately have no paste.
3. **Factory:** place 0805/1206/1210 passives, diodes and ICs. Verify C1 polarity, TVS/diode cathodes, pin 1 on each IC and the exact latch-off TPS2553DBVR-1 suffix. U8 TPS61085DGKR uses the supplied 0.65 mm-pitch VSSOP footprint. Confirm D4 cathode orientation and U4 TPS2553DBVR-1 and U9 TPS259620DDAR, including the exposed ground pad and six adjacent thermal vias. D10 must be OPSCO SK6805-EC15 with the supplied pin mapping; U10 is SN74AHCT1G125DBVR. Do not substitute a different addressable LED footprint. There are no components on the bottom.
4. **User, after delivery:** inspect joints and measure for supply shorts. Install U5 first, before the tall connectors. Buy exact STM32F042K6T6 / LQFP32 from DigiKey. Match its pin-one mark to the [MCU orientation map](../output/documentation/MCU_hand_solder_map.pdf), align all 32 legs, tack opposite corners, recheck alignment, then flux/solder the remaining leads with an iron. Wick bridges, clean and inspect all leads under magnification. There is no central exposed pad. A full-board stencil cannot sit over the already-fitted SMT parts; whole-board hot-plate reflow is not required. Hand solder the six horizontal Weidmüller terminals after SMT reflow as the user assembly step. Fit J8, the recessed female 2×5 2.54 mm service socket, with pin 1 at the square pad. Then fit JP101/JP201/JP301: 2 mm headers on the top side, straight and fully seated, with pin 1 at the square pad. Use a removable cap temporarily as an alignment aid with the board unpowered; remove it afterward. Fill both plated holes per terminal pole. Support the board without loading fresh SMT joints. The terminal pins extend below the board.
5. Leave all selectors open for initial bring-up. Supply J1 from a current-limited bench supply, starting at 12 V and a modest current limit. Confirm input polarity, protected input, approximately 5.07 V internal, 3.3 V logic and approximately 12 V at the raw BOOST_12V reservoir. With a blank MCU, protected sensor rails should remain disabled; do not expect normal LED or CAN operation until programming. Sensor headers remain unpowered while power selectors are open.
6. Program the combined factory image through the custom J8 mapping, set/verify boot protection, power cycle and configure a unique CAN ID using [CAN service](CAN_SERVICE.md). Follow the [fixture procedure](FIXTURE.md). Save each input mode in software and verify readback. Then power off, choose each port's supply and termination settings, inspect one-cap-per-header placement, and check that 5 V and 12 V are not shorted.
7. Perform the [qualification checks](VALIDATION.md) before connecting a robot harness. Begin with a known load and known logic source, then the actual sensor/cable.

Use M3 hardware at (3.5,4) and (48.5,46.5) mm. Holes are 3.2 mm NPTH. The copper keepout has at least 3.30 mm radius; use hardware whose contact area stays within a 6.4 mm diameter, with insulating washers/spacers as appropriate. Account for component bodies, connector overhang and protruding pins. Do not clamp the PCB between conductive surfaces. The [mechanical drawing](../output/mechanical/mechanical_drawing.pdf) gives nominal geometry, not a fitted enclosure.

U5 is the only SMT part assigned to the user. The other ICs, VSSOP, RGB LED and passives remain factory assembled. Inspect the user-soldered 32 MCU leads and 49 through-hole joints before power-up. See jumper retention and vibration qualification in [WIRING.md](WIRING.md).

# sugarCANe underside artwork

[sugarCANe.svg](sugarCANe.svg) is the original vector master, 52 × 74 mm, viewed from the underside. The white artwork spans approximately 45.6 × 45.8 mm between the connector fields. Three curved, jointed cane stalks fan into sweeping leaves with open veins; a shallow convex hill rises beneath their roots. A large, soft-serif **sugarCANe** wordmark sits below, with a heavier **CAN**. There are no borders, pixel accents or additional text on the underside.

The illustration is original vector artwork. No Minecraft texture, logo or font is included. The soft-serif lettering uses **Fraunces**, weight 650 for the lowercase letters and 750 for CAN, by the Fraunces Project Authors. The optical-size axis is 72, softness is 70 and WONK is enabled. All lettering is converted to filled outlines. SC12 Rev H remains the hardware identity on the component side and in the engineering files.

Font source: [Google Fonts, pinned commit 4024282](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/4024282d9b0cffcdb8e3024560862746178d741f/ofl/fraunces/Fraunces%5BSOFT%2CWONK%2Copsz%2Cwght%5D.ttf). The unmodified [variable font file](../../sources/fonts/Fraunces.ttf) and its [SIL Open Font License 1.1](../../licenses/Fraunces-OFL.txt) are retained. Font SHA-256: `177ff6c0f14e5550a3c624247cd1189611d4eb65d000b14944c63d967958abbb`.

Rebuild the vector source with `python scripts/build_brand_artwork.py` from `current/`. The generator resolves overlapping font contours with nonzero winding and flattens the original Bezier curves into fine polygons (0.004 mm illustration tolerance; 0.35 font units for lettering). Apply it with `kicad-python scripts/apply_bottom_artwork.py`. The importer mirrors the artwork once into native PCB coordinates and uses KiCad's filled cut-contour polygons to preserve letter and leaf holes without a font dependency or raster image. `finish_markings.py` also reapplies this artwork after refreshing the top labels.

All artwork lives on B.Silkscreen, in the **sugarCANe artwork** group. It changes no copper, solder mask, holes, pads, components or top markings. Refresh the bottom silkscreen Gerber, bottom drawing, interactive BOM and render after editing; check the actual exported mask clearances and final image. Small via holes remain visible through the artwork in the CAD render. Electrical qualification status is unchanged.

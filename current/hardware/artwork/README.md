# sugarCANe underside artwork

[sugarCANe.svg](sugarCANe.svg) is the original vector master, 52 × 74 mm, viewed from the underside. The white artwork spans approximately 45.6 × 44.4 mm between the connector fields. The original three jointed pixel stalks and stair-step leaves stand on a stepped convex mound. A large **sugarCANe** wordmark sits below, with a heavier **CAN**. There are no corner decorations, borders or additional text on the underside.

The illustration is original vector artwork. No Minecraft texture, logo or font is included. The lettering uses **IBM Plex Sans**, weight 600 for the lowercase letters and 700 for CAN, at its normal width. Its smooth outlines, clear crossbar and open aperture make the final lowercase **e** distinct from the pixel illustration. All lettering is converted to filled outlines. SC12 Rev H remains the hardware identity on the component side and in the engineering files.

Font source: [Google Fonts, pinned commit 0b58fb3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/0b58fb370093f9a9f4ff785d94405710b79de67c/ofl/ibmplexsans/IBMPlexSans%5Bwdth%2Cwght%5D.ttf). The unmodified [variable font file](../../sources/fonts/IBMPlexSans.ttf) and its [SIL Open Font License 1.1](../../licenses/IBMPlexSans-OFL.txt) are retained. Font SHA-256: `3b031aa4216174205bd8471f88a49b91f093169e9e87bd5262242bc5967fe2e3`.

Rebuild the vector source with `python scripts/build_brand_artwork.py` from `current/`. The illustration uses a 0.8 mm pixel grid. The generator resolves overlapping font contours with nonzero winding and flattens the lettering curves into fine polygons with a 0.35-font-unit tolerance. Apply it with `kicad-python scripts/apply_bottom_artwork.py`. The importer mirrors the artwork once into native PCB coordinates and uses KiCad's filled cut-contour polygons to preserve letter and stalk holes without a font dependency or raster image. `finish_markings.py` also reapplies this artwork after refreshing the top labels.

All artwork lives on B.Silkscreen, in the **sugarCANe artwork** group. It changes no copper, solder mask, holes, pads, components or top markings. Refresh the bottom silkscreen Gerber, bottom drawing, interactive BOM and render after editing; check the actual exported mask clearances and final image. Small via holes remain visible through the artwork in the CAD render. Electrical qualification status is unchanged.

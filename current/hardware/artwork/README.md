# sugarCANe underside artwork

[sugarCANe.svg](sugarCANe.svg) is the original vector master, 52 × 74 mm, viewed from the underside. The white artwork spans approximately 45.6 × 45.5 mm between the connector fields. Three curved, jointed cane stalks fan into sweeping leaves with open veins; one broad curved stroke gathers their roots. A large, smooth **sugarCANe** wordmark sits below, with a heavier **CAN**. There are no borders, pixel accents or additional text on the underside.

The illustration is original vector artwork. No Minecraft texture, logo or font is included. The readable geometric lettering uses **Outfit**, weight 550 for the lowercase letters and 800 for CAN, by the Outfit Project Authors. All lettering is converted to filled outlines. SC12 Rev H remains the hardware identity on the component side and in the engineering files.

Font source: [Google Fonts, pinned commit 8b0a1d0](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/8b0a1d0f5983c89bc2b93f1b5fb55f9e252744b5/ofl/outfit/Outfit%5Bwght%5D.ttf). The unmodified [variable font file](../../sources/fonts/Outfit.ttf) and its [SIL Open Font License 1.1](../../licenses/Outfit-OFL.txt) are retained. Font SHA-256: `fc7287273e66929776e2ba54f144fe699080bec29f61bf649d70d871468aeade`.

Rebuild the vector source with `python scripts/build_brand_artwork.py` from `current/`. The generator resolves overlapping font contours with nonzero winding and flattens the original Bezier curves into fine polygons (0.004 mm illustration tolerance; 0.35 font units for lettering). Apply it with `kicad-python scripts/apply_bottom_artwork.py`. The importer mirrors the artwork once into native PCB coordinates and uses KiCad's filled cut-contour polygons to preserve letter and leaf holes without a font dependency or raster image. `finish_markings.py` also reapplies this artwork after refreshing the top labels.

All artwork lives on B.Silkscreen, in the **sugarCANe artwork** group. It changes no copper, solder mask, holes, pads, components or top markings. Refresh the bottom silkscreen Gerber, bottom drawing, interactive BOM and render after editing; check the actual exported mask clearances and final image. Small via holes remain visible through the artwork in the CAD render. Electrical qualification status is unchanged.

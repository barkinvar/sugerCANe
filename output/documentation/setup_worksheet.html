Install one shunt at most on each power or bias selector. Disconnect board power before changing jumpers. JP1 is momentary reset; leave it open. JP2 is CAN termination; fit it only at a physical end of the trunk.

Connection limits

  • Each sensor connector is pin 1 POWER, pin 2 GND, pin 3 SIGNAL. The board targets 0–30 V positive signals and a 6–18 V protected board supply.
  • Battery power follows the robot battery. A sensor requiring 10 V can fail during a battery sag while this interface continues running.
  • Logic mode targets LOW ≤0.8 V and HIGH ≥2.4 V. Industrial mode targets LOW ≤4 V and HIGH ≥7 V.
  • Industrial NPN/PNP examples assume ≤0.5 mA off leakage. The 3.3 V open-drain pull-up example assumes ≤50 µA. Check the actual sensor datasheet.
  • External supply voltage must never enter a POWER pin. Externally powered 24 V sensors need the wiring arrangements in the full guide.

This worksheet performs arithmetic and selects documented modes. It does not establish sensor compatibility, measured electrical ratings, or robot inspection approval.

Sum of entered sensor currents and conservative bias-load allowances. The 300 mA budget applies to each complete rail, shared by all four ports. Startup current requires a separate bench check.

Before enabling power
Verify polarity, shunts and both rail voltages with a current-limited supply. Assign a unique ID before connecting to the robot CAN bus. Use the supplied robot driver’s stale-data checks and explicit fault recovery.

# Rev D-2 SC12 assembly

Factory: fit only the 98 SMT references in [Factory_BOM.csv](../bom/Factory_BOM.csv) using [CPL_JLCPCB.csv](../output/assembly/CPL_JLCPCB.csv). User: buy the [nine through-hole parts](../bom/Hand_solder_BOM.csv) and [three closed-top caps](../bom/ACCESSORIES.csv).

Use the exact [BOM](../bom/BOM_grouped.csv), [assembly drawing](../output/assembly/top_assembly.pdf), [interactive BOM](../output/assembly/interactive_bom.html) and placement files. Reference labels are in these drawings; dense placement does not leave room to print every reference legibly on the actual top silkscreen.

1. Order the 52 ×64 mm, four-layer, 1.6 mm board with 1 oz finished copper target. Use the exported Gerbers and separate plated/nonplated drill files. Have the fabricator review its actual stackup, annular rings, mask registration and minimum features. Gold finish is useful at the programming contacts; keep solder and paste off them.
2. Check bare-board continuity and orientation. Apply paste to SMT pads only. A 0.10 mm stencil is a starting point; the assembler must review release and the exact QFN apertures. Through-hole header pads, solder selectors, TC2050 contacts and the test point deliberately have no paste.
3. Place 0402/0805/1206/1210 passives, diodes and ICs. Verify C1 polarity, TVS/diode cathodes, pin 1 on each IC and the exact latch-off TPS2553DBVR-1 suffix. U8 TPS552872-Q1 has five long electrical power lands and fine-pitch perimeter pads; it is not a generic single-exposed-pad package. Use its supplied paste pattern, a controlled reflow profile and suitable joint inspection. There are no components on the bottom.
4. Inspect joints and measure for supply shorts. Hand solder the six horizontal Weidmüller terminals after SMT reflow as the user assembly step. Then fit JP101/JP201/JP301: 2 mm headers on the top side, straight and fully seated, with pin 1 at the square pad. Use a removable cap temporarily as an alignment aid with the board unpowered; remove it afterward. Fill both plated holes per terminal pole. Support the board without loading fresh SMT joints. The terminal pins extend below the board.
5. Leave all selectors open for initial bring-up. Supply J1 from a current-limited bench supply, starting at 12 V and a modest current limit. Confirm input polarity, protected input, approximately 5.07 V internal, 3.3 V logic and 12.01 V regulated rails. Sensor headers remain unpowered while power selectors are open.
6. Program through the custom TC2050 mapping, verify flash and configure a unique CAN ID over UART. Then power off, choose each port's supply/bias and termination settings, inspect the one-cap-per-header settings and bias bridges, and check that 5 V and 12 V are not shorted.
7. Perform the [qualification checks](VALIDATION.md) before connecting a robot harness. Begin with a known load and known logic source, then the actual sensor/cable.

Use M3 hardware at (3.5,4) and (48.5,42.5) mm. Holes are 3.2 mm NPTH. The copper keepout has at least 3.30 mm radius; use hardware whose contact area stays within a 6.4 mm diameter, with insulating washers/spacers as appropriate. Account for component bodies, connector overhang and protruding pins. Do not clamp the PCB between conductive surfaces. The [mechanical drawing](../output/mechanical/mechanical_drawing.pdf) gives nominal geometry, not a fitted enclosure.

The compact board still benefits from magnification and a fine iron. Rev D-2 restores removable voltage shunts and retains large passives where possible. See the retention and vibration qualification notes in [WIRING.md](WIRING.md). The buck-boost QFN and four 0402 capacitors make stencil/reflow work more demanding than Rev C; it is not a through-hole beginner kit.
